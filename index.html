02
Case competition · Nov 2024 · 2nd of 72 teams

Poogle

A smart device that attaches to any toilet and uses sensors and AI to catch early signs of digestive disease in adults over 65. We placed 2nd of 72 teams (400+ participants) at the Case Competition hosted by Google & Foothill College.

  • Problem: 40% of adults over 65 have an ongoing digestive complaint, but checks are rare and invasive
  • Market: sized from a $37.6B global category to a $601.6M U.S. segment and a $30M first target
  • Business model: $1,065 device at a 50% margin, a subscription for insights, and B2B channels through senior care and insurers
  • Format: two written rounds and a video round, then a live final pitch with a three-year roadmap
